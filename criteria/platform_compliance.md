# Platform Compliance Rules

## 小红书 (Xiaohongshu)
- Max 1000 characters for caption
- Max 18 images per post
- Hashtags: recommended 5-10, no strict limit
- Links in caption are restricted (only shop links)
- No QR codes or WeChat IDs in images

## Twitter/X
- Max 280 characters per post (for basic accounts)
- Max 4 images per post
- Max 1 video per post
- Hashtags: 1-3 recommended, too many looks spammy
- Links count as 23 characters

## LinkedIn
- Max 3000 characters for posts
- Max 9 images per post
- Professional content only — no casual/personal posts
- Hashtags: 3-5 recommended
- Article format available for longer content

## 知乎 (Zhihu)
- Max 10000 characters for answers
- Supports rich text formatting
- Links allowed but should be relevant
- No commercial promotion without disclosure

## YouTube
- Max 5000 characters for description
- Links in description: first 2-3 are most visible
- Hashtags: first 3 appear above title
- Timestamps are encouraged

## TikTok
- Max 2200 characters for caption
- Hashtags: 3-5 recommended
- Links in bio only (not caption for most accounts)
- Trending sounds/effects boost reach

## General Notes
- Always check the latest platform rules (they change frequently)
- Character counts include spaces and punctuation
- Chinese characters count as 1 each on all platforms
