# Safety & Compliance Criteria

## Prohibited Content (→ fail)

- Hate speech, discrimination, or harassment
- Violence, threats, or incitement to violence
- Self-harm or suicide content
- Illegal activities or instructions
- CSAM or exploitation of minors
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Clearly plagiarized content from paywalled sources

## Sensitive Content (→ flag or fail depending on context)

### Political/Social Issues
- Direct political commentary on sensitive topics
- Unverified social unrest claims
- Content that could be interpreted as challenging core values

### Health & Safety
- Unverified medical claims (especially COVID/vaccine related)
- Dangerous "challenges" or stunts
- Unregulated health products

### Financial
- Investment advice without qualification
- Pyramid schemes or get-rich-quick content
- Unlicensed financial services

## Copyright
- Direct copying from news articles without transformation
- Using copyrighted images without attribution
- Republishing gated/paywalled content

## Privacy
- Personal phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers
- Screenshots of private conversations without consent
- Photos of non-public individuals without permission

## Risk Level Assessment
- Low: Minor concern, unlikely to cause harm
- Medium: Potential for misunderstanding or offense
- High: Clear violation that could result in content removal or account suspension
