# Style Guidelines

## Platform-Specific Voice

### 小红书 (Xiaohongshu)
- Warm, personal, conversational tone
- Use emoji naturally (📕🌟💡)
- Short paragraphs, scannable format
- Authentic and relatable voice
- Include personal experience/opinion

### Twitter/X
- Concise, punchy, quotable
- Thread format for longer content
- Hook in first sentence
- Hashtags: 1-3 max
- English or bilingual OK

### LinkedIn
- Professional but not stuffy
- Clear value proposition upfront
- Industry insights and actionable takeaways
- Longer-form is acceptable
- Minimal emoji, professional tone

### 知乎 (Zhihu)
- Analytical, in-depth
- Evidence-based arguments
- Professional/academic tone
- Structured with clear sections

### YouTube / TikTok
- Attention-grabbing opening
- Visual-friendly descriptions
- Call to action at end
- Casual, energetic tone

## General Rules
- No clickbait titles
- Consistent voice throughout
- Proper paragraph breaks
- No excessive capitalization or punctuation
