# Fact Check Criteria

## Core Principles

1. All factual claims must be verifiable against authoritative sources.
2. Statistics and data points must cite specific, identifiable sources.
3. Claims about scientific research must reference actual studies (not "studies show" without specifics).
4. Historical claims must be chronologically accurate.
5. Health/medical claims require extra scrutiny — flag anything not from established medical consensus.

## Red Flags (→ fail)

- Clearly fabricated statistics or numbers
- Claims that directly contradict widely-known facts
- Medical advice without qualification or source
- Misrepresented quotes or data
- Conspiracy theories presented as fact

## Warning Signs (→ flag)

- "Studies show" without any specific citation
- Vague data ("many people", "most experts") without quantification
- Outdated statistics (check if data is from >5 years ago without context)
- Overgeneralization from a single study or data point
- Percentages without base numbers

## Special Notes for Chinese Content

- Be aware of commonly-circulated myths on Chinese social media
- Cross-reference with Chinese-language authoritative sources when possible
- Pay attention to translated content accuracy
