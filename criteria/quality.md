# Quality Assessment Criteria

## Scoring Rubric (1-10)

### Completeness
- 9-10: Topic fully covered, all angles addressed, reader needs no external context
- 7-8: Most aspects covered, minor gaps
- 5-6: Core idea present but significant gaps
- 3-4: Superficial treatment
- 1-2: Incomplete, feels cut off

### Coherence
- 9-10: Logical flow, clear structure, smooth transitions
- 7-8: Generally well-structured, occasional awkward jumps
- 5-6: Readable but disjointed
- 3-4: Hard to follow
- 1-2: Incoherent

### Originality
- 9-10: Unique angle, fresh insights, novel perspective
- 7-8: Good synthesis with some original thought
- 5-6: Competent but derivative
- 3-4: Generic, feels AI-generated
- 1-2: Plagiarized or completely unoriginal

### Information Density
- 9-10: Every sentence carries weight, no filler
- 7-8: Mostly substantive, minimal fluff
- 5-6: Some useful content mixed with filler
- 3-4: Mostly filler
- 1-2: Empty

### Engagement
- 9-10: Compelling, thought-provoking, shareable
- 7-8: Interesting, holds attention
- 5-6: Readable but forgettable
- 3-4: Boring
- 1-2: Unreadable

## Minimum Threshold

Content scoring below 7 overall should be rejected for publishing without substantial revision.
